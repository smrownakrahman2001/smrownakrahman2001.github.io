01 / About

Grounded in classrooms. Focused on systems.

I graduated from the University of Dhaka in World Religions and Culture and am pursuing postgraduate study in Educational Leadership and School Improvement at BRAC University–IED. My professional work connects direct classroom experience with school improvement, programme delivery, stakeholder coordination and evidence-informed decisions.

Through Teach For Bangladesh, I have worked in a large government primary school in Keraniganj, while leading or contributing to financial literacy, school health, parent engagement and teacher learning initiatives. 02 / Selected work

Selected work across learning, community health, teacher development and education innovation. Outcomes are distinguished from activities, estimates and work in progress.

Financial literacy · 2025–2026

Ek Taka, Ek Shopno

A school-based financial literacy initiative connecting basic money concepts, budgeting and saving habits with child-friendly learning activities.

Learning designFinancial literacyAssessment
Reported project outcome: 90% of 65 participating students achieved at least 75% activity completion. This is a completion metric, not evidence of long-term saving behaviour.
Read the case study

Challenge: Children need age-appropriate opportunities to understand everyday financial decisions.

My contribution: Designed activities including a story-based introduction, budget-market role-play, weekly budget sheet and Saving Heroes Club; coordinated stakeholder participation and collected learning evidence.

Research extension: Exploring intervention and comparison groups across Keraniganj and Rangpur. Findings should not be presented as causal until the design and analysis support that interpretation.

Next evidence to publish: Anonymized assessment summary, activity photos with permission, questionnaire and final research report.
