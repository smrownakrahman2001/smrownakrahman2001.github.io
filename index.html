01 — Profile

About

I'm a graduate of the University of Dhaka (B.A. Hons, World Religions and Culture) and hold an M.Ed. in Educational Leadership and Administration from BRAC University. My work sits at the overlap of three things I care about: classrooms that actually serve the children in front of them, research that's grounded in lived community context, and program systems that are honest about whether they're working.

As a Teaching Fellow with Teach For Bangladesh, I teach in an underserved primary school context — and as a Researcher within the same fellowship, I designed and ran a community financial literacy initiative from planning through delivery, including stakeholder reporting and progress documentation.

Before this, I worked across HR, youth social entrepreneurship programs, and academic research — experience that's given me a practical view of how organizations run, not just how programs are designed on paper. I'm now building toward roles in program coordination, MEAL, and education-focused development work with NGOs and INGOs.
